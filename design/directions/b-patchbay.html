B · PATCH BAY

Studio hardware for your storage. Drives are rack modules, apps are patched into them with cables. Pull a drive and you see exactly which cables fall loose.

Mood

Tactile, precise, a little nerdy — a well-kept recording studio. Graphite faceplates, LED meters and one bright cable colour per drive. The map is the hero: dependency is something you can see and touch.

Motion

  • Cables are spring-simulated (verlet, 12 points): they sway when hovered and stretch when tugged.
  • Unplugging a drive drops its cables with gravity and a small bounce; reconnecting snaps plugs home with a click haptic.
  • LEDs blink with real read/write activity; capacity meters fill segment by segment.
  • Reduced Motion: cables are static curves, state changes crossfade.
