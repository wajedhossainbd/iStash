A · MANIFEST

A ship's manifest for your data. Every folder is a crate: weighed, labelled, stamped when it's verified, and always on the list so you can bring it home.

Mood

Calm logistics. Warm paper and hull ink, one signal orange used only for the thing that needs you. Feels like a careful quartermaster, not a cleaner shouting about junk.

Motion

  • Crates ride a dashed rail from MAC to the drive while copying; a slight bob, never a spin.
  • Verify ends in a stamp — a 120 ms "thump" scale 1.15 → 1 with a tiny rotation settle.
  • Sizes count up in mono with tabular figures; the free-space bar slides as crates leave.
  • Reduced Motion: crates jump, stamps fade.

Why it fits

Stowaway = cargo. The journal becomes the manifest; trust is shown as stamps and counts, not promises.
