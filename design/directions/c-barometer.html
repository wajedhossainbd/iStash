C · Barometer

Your Mac's weather. Stowaway speaks in plain-language forecasts — "memory runs out in about 38 minutes" — read from instruments with a brass set-hand showing where you were.

Mood

Editorial and calm, like a good weather page or a ship's instrument panel. Paper, hairline rules, a serif for the sentence that matters, mono for every number. Colour only means condition: calm, busy, strained, critical.

Motion

  • The needle is a damped spring (slight overshoot, settles in ~600 ms); the brass hand trails it by 20 minutes.
  • The forecast cone draws in from "now" and gently widens/narrows as confidence changes.
  • "Weather turning": the page's corner wash crossfades to the new condition colour over 1.2 s; the headline swaps word by word.
  • Reduced Motion: needle jumps, washes swap instantly.
